## The Comet Toolbox

The Comet Toolbox allows users to efficiently implement Multiverse Analyses and is focused, but not limited to, neuroimaging research. 
In brief, Multiverse Analysis is a statistical approach proposed to aid the reproduciblity crisis by running not only one but many different analysis pipelines. 
This can then serve as a robustness check over analytical decisions or serve as an exploratory tool to assess patterns in the results space of an analysis.
