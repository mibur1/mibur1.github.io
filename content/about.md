Hi, I'm Micha. I'm a PhD student in the
[Psychological Methods and Statistics Lab](https://uol.de/en/psychology/psychological-methods-and-statistics)
at Carl von Ossietzky Universität Oldenburg, where I work on network neuroscience in individual
differences research and on making the analysis of neuroimaging data more robust.
More info can be found under [Research](/research/).

I try to incorporate open science principles in all my work. I am a
member of the [Open Science Interest Group](https://osig-uol.github.io/), and my
software and [teaching materials](/teaching/) are all public on
[GitHub](https://github.com/mibur1).

In my free time I climb, teach bouldering groups for kids, and spend as much
time outdoors and travelling as the calendar allows.

I'm always happy to chat, feel free to reach out via
[mail](mailto:micha.burkhardt@uol.de).
